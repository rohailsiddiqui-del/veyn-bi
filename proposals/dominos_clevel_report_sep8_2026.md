## 7. Executive Recommendations

### Immediate (Today / This Week)

1. **Food safety escalation** — Hair-in-pizza and raw pizza incidents from Sep 8. Operations team to trace which branch and open a formal food safety review.
2. **Fire threat call review** — One customer threatened to "set the office on fire." QA + security team to review the recording. Confirm no further action needed.
3. **Police station complaint** — Customer stated they filed a police complaint over non-delivery post-payment. Legal/compliance to confirm if a formal complaint was received.
4. **EasyPaisa payment failures** — Multiple customers paying via EasyPaisa and receiving no order. Escalate to tech team for integration audit — direct financial liability.
5. **Coach Awais Babar immediately** (Order Taking, 45.7) — Zero pass rate on Customer Name, Effective Listening, and Closing Greetings. Do not allow unsupervised calls until reviewed and remediated.

### Short-Term (This Month)

6. **Pending call SLA** — 47.8% of complaint inbound calls are Pending. Introduce mandatory same-shift callbacks for all Pending outcomes before agents clock off.
7. **Apology & Empathy training (Complaint Inbound)** — These two parameters fail on 31–35% of calls across ALL 6 agents — this is a team-wide training gap. A focused 1-day workshop on apology language and empathy scripting would lift every agent's score.
8. **Script compliance (Order Taking)** — Suggestions, Menu Conclude, and Promotion failing on 70–77% of calls. Supervisors to run a script refresher session and monitor compliance daily for 2 weeks. Customer Name (50% fail) is also unacceptable — every agent must address the customer by name.
9. **Coach Raja Hamza** (Complaint Inbound, 73.8) — Specific gaps: Apology 50%, Ending 54%. Pair with Usama Faiz (91.8) for peer mentoring.
10. **App/website reliability escalation** — "App not working," "invalid parameters," "website not opening" appear across multiple calls. Losing orders daily — needs a dedicated product team ticket.
11. **Callback discipline** — Customers repeatedly stating they called before with no callback. Implement tracking + mandatory confirmation before a case is marked closed.

### Strategic (Next Quarter)

10. **Negative → Neutral conversion target** — Complaint Inbound's 42.2% negative sentiment rate needs to drop to below 30% by Q4. Set this as a KPI for the complaint team.
11. **Complaint resolution rate target** — Current 28.9% is too low. Target 50% true resolution rate by end of Q4 — means fewer Pending, more closed outcomes.
12. **Real-time signal alerts** — Enable same-day Veyn AI alerts for threat/regulatory/social media signals so supervisors can intervene before a call becomes a public complaint or legal risk.
